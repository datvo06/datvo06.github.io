What are predicates, operators, and skills?

The running example: a kitchen with a jug, a faucet, a burner.

Predicate: a named true/false fact about the state
HandEmpty ✓ JugAtFaucet(jug) ✓ FaucetOn ✗ WaterBoiled ✗
Read off the raw state, they abstract where things are into a handful of symbols.
Operator: an action as preconditions and effects
PickJugFromFaucet(robot, jug)
  pre: HandEmpty · JugAtFaucet(jug)
  add: Holding(jug)
  del: HandEmpty · JugAtFaucet(jug)
When its preconditions hold, it can fire; its effects rewrite the predicates. They abstract what actions do.
Skill: the controller that executes an operator
The same PickJugFromFaucet, one level down: a motion controller with continuous parameters (the grasp pose, the approach path). The operator names it; the skill runs it.

The question: how should a robot build its own abstract world model?

Symbolic skeleton
operators chain the predicates toward the goal: HandEmpty → Holding(jug) → JugAtBurner → WaterBoiled
↓ refine
Continuous refinement
sample skill parameters against a simulator until the skeleton is executable

Bilevel planning. Every iteration keeps this frame and changes what the abstraction can express.

Act 1 · VisualPredicator (ICLR 2025)

Static tabletop, raw images, no demonstrations

Blocks, coffee, balance: the environments as the robot sees them (VisualPredicator, arXiv:2410.23156).

The world is static: it changes only when the robot acts. No demonstrations: the agent explores, planning with what it has and executing.
Given
The skills, , and an initial predicate set \(\Psi_0\): usually just the goal predicates \(\Psi_G\) (Blocks: On, OnTable).
Invented
Every other predicate: proposed as Python code by a VLM from the agent's own data.
Learned
The operators: preconditions and effects over those predicates.
The missing piece. The predicates a planner reasons over must come from perception, not from hand-fed features. Who writes On(a, b) or Clear(x) from pixels?

Neuro-symbolic predicates, invented from the agent's own data

A predicate is classifier code over perception
class NSPredicate:
    # a VLM answers the classifier over raw visual state
    def holds(self, state, objs) -> bool:
        return vlm_query(
            state.image,
            f"Is {objs[0]} inside {objs[1]}?")
# proposed as Python code, not read from features
The invention loop. From the agent's exploration data, a VLM proposes candidate predicates as code, three prompts: when does a skill apply, what changed after it ran, and logical extensions of existing predicates. A greedy search from the goal predicates keeps the best-scoring set; operators are learned over the survivors.
kept vs discarded: VLM proposals have low precision, so the search prunes them, by classification accuracy of the learned operators early on, by planning utility once satisficing plans exist.

What we get, and the limit

Captures. Solves static manipulation from images: predicates and operators, learned, no hand-fed features.
Limit. Effects are instantaneous, and nothing happens unless the robot acts. A kettle that boils on its own is not representable.

Act 2 · ExoPredicator (ICLR 2026) · unchanged: bilevel planning, invention, skills

The world changes on its own

Boil water with jugs: the water fills and heats while the robot is busy elsewhere.

Turn the faucet on, and the jug keeps filling for several steps. Turn the burner on, and the water boils later. Instantaneous-effect operators cannot say that.

One new object: the causal process

process =
conditions at-start · overall · at-end
effects add / delete atoms
delay a sampled distribution
strength a learnable weight
Endogenous: initiated by a skill. Exogenous: fires on its own once its conditions hold.
the fuse timeline
FaucetOn  ___/‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾  (rising edge: trigger)
delay          |<-- sampled -->|
JugFilled ________________/‾‾‾  (effect lands...)
overall   ‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾  (...only if it survived)

Trigger on a rising edge, wait a sampled delay, and the effect lands only if the overall condition held through the window. Break it early (turn the faucet off) and the effect is cancelled.

Planning and learning with processes

planning becomes temporal
The world model schedules effects at sampled delays and cancels them if the overall condition breaks. Wait becomes a first-class skill, so an exogenous fuse can burn down while the robot does nothing.
learning the processes
Structure from clustering plus LLM selection; the delay and strength parameters by MAP fitting on the trajectories.

What we get, and the limit

Captures. Durative, delayed, exogenous change. Benchmark wins on boil / fan / domino envs over ViLa zero and few-shot, and a no-invent oracle.
Limit. The model is symbolic only. It says JugFilled flips a few steps after the faucet turns on, but not the law volume += rate, so refinement still leans on a ground-truth simulator it did not learn.

Who writes the simulator?

We know the rigid-body part, physics gives it to us. The process part, fill rates and heat thresholds, is exactly what we do not know. So factor the dynamics.
trusted rigid-body base sim
+
learned rules (overwrite only declared process features)
=
full simulator; the base-sim residual is the learning target

Two files, one set of constants

simulator.py · process dynamics
def fill_rule(s, p):
    if s.faucet_on:
        s.water = min(s.water + p.fill_rate,
                      p.jug_full_level)
    return s
predicates.py · threshold classifiers
def JugFilled(s, p):
    return s.water >= p.jug_full_level
params
fill_rate · jug_full_level · heat_rate · boil_temp
Consistency by construction. JugFilled thresholds on the very jug_full_level that fill_rule saturates toward. The question "will the predicate and the dynamics disagree" cannot arise: they are the same numbers.

Fitting is Bayesian system identification

$$ p(\theta \mid \mathcal{D}) \;\propto\; \exp\!\left(-\tfrac{1}{2\sigma^2}\textstyle\sum_t \lVert x_{t+1} - f_\theta(x_t) \rVert^2\right)\, p(\theta) $$

A sum-of-squared-errors likelihood over the residual, a boxed prior on each named constant. Fit with emcee MCMC or a Levenberg-Marquardt MAP with Laplace covariance.

Identifiability. Sloppy directions surface honestly: where the data is silent, the posterior stays wide and says so.

Planning as validation. The slow gate is not one-step error. It is: can the planner refine a full sketch under your model, then forward-validate it? Snapshots are versioned so a regression can roll back.

Active experiments

The posterior turns each atom into a Bernoulli. Ensemble disagreement, the mean binary entropy across posterior draws, steers exploration toward the states where the model is least sure.
